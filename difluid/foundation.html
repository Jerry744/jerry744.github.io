02 / BRAND FOUNDATION品牌建立逻辑

Coffee,
understood.读懂咖啡。

Every product, data point, and interaction should help users understand coffee a little better.每一次产品使用、数据呈现和品牌沟通，都应该帮助用户多理解一点咖啡。

Our brand promise. A useful question for every decision.品牌承诺，也是每一次决策的共同起点。

01 / THE BELIEF核心信念

Tools extend perception.
Judgment stays human.工具扩展感知，
判断始终由人完成。

01

Equipment设备

Make change observable.让变化可以被观察。

02

Data数据

Make differences comparable.让差异可以被比较。

03

Knowledge知识

Make experience shareable.让经验可以被交流。

Human understanding人的理解

Taste. Interpret. Decide.品尝，解释，形成自己的判断。

A number becomes useful when it meets a process, a condition, and a person's experience.当数字与过程、条件和人的感受相连，它才成为理解咖啡的线索。

02 / THE PRODUCT LOGIC产品建立逻辑

One product.
One more dimension.每一款产品，
多理解一个维度。

01

What becomes visible?让什么变得可见？

Start with a state or change that is hard to observe.从一个难以观察的状态或变化出发。

02

What can be compared?让什么可以比较？

Keep the method, conditions, and context close to the result.让方法、条件与语境始终伴随结果。

03

What can a person learn?帮助人理解什么？

Connect the observation to a question worth exploring.把观察结果连接到一个值得继续探索的问题。

Together, products should build a more connected understanding of coffee.产品之间建立联系，共同形成更完整的咖啡理解。

03 / THE DIRECTION面向未来的品牌方向

Make understanding
as natural as tasting.让理解咖啡，
像品尝咖啡一样自然。

WHY

Experience deserves to be observed, shared, and carried forward.让经验可以被观察、交流，并持续积累。

HOW

Connect products, data, and knowledge around the questions people ask.围绕人的问题，连接产品、数据与知识。

TOWARD

People who see more, compare clearly, and develop their own judgment.让人看见更多，比较得更清楚，逐步形成自己的判断。

A direction for future decisions across products, design, communication, and support.这一方向将持续指导产品、设计、传播与用户支持。

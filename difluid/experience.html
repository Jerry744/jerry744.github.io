05 / Experience system体验系统

From a number.
To a next step.从一个数字，到下一步行动。

An experience should leave you with a clearer understanding—and the confidence to act.一次好的体验，应该让你理解得更清楚，也更有信心采取行动。

Experience direction · Working proposal体验方向 · 前瞻提案

01 / Continuity连续性

One task.
Across every touchpoint.同一个任务，贯穿每一个触点。

A measurement becomes more useful when its context travels with it.当上下文被保留，测量才更有用。

  1. 01

    Device设备

    Make the invisible observable.让不可见的状态变得可观察。

    A measurement + its conditions一次测量 + 测量条件
  2. 02

    Software软件

    Put the reading in context.把读数放回具体情境。

    A session + its history一次任务 + 历史记录
  3. 03

    Knowledge知识

    Turn a difference into a question.将差异转化为值得探索的问题。

    An explanation + a next step一种解释 + 下一步行动
  4. 04

    Service服务

    Keep understanding moving.让理解继续发生。

    Shared context + a recovery path共享上下文 + 恢复路径

Illustrative journey · A design direction, not a statement of current product integrations.体验链路示意 · 表达设计方向，不代表当前产品已实现这些集成。

02 / Understanding Loop理解循环

Design the path
to understanding.设计通往理解的路径。

Select a stage to explore its question, output and checkpoint.选择一个阶段，查看它要回答的问题、产物与检查点。

01 / 07 · Anchor

Whose uncertainty are we reducing?我们在为谁减少哪一种不确定？

The output产物

An intent brief一页体验意图

Name the person, the task, the desired change and the evidence we still need.明确使用者、任务、期待的变化，以及仍需验证的证据。

The checkpoint检查点

A real user. A meaningful next step. A clear owner.有具体用户、有意义的下一步，也有明确的负责人。

02 / 07 · Understand

What is getting in the way?是什么阻碍了用户？

The output产物

A user & task evidence map用户与任务证据图

Observe real work. Separate what we know from what we assume.观察真实任务，区分已知事实与待验证的假设。

The checkpoint检查点

Can the problem be described and tested in a real context?能否在真实情境中描述并验证这个问题？

03 / 07 · Map

Where does understanding break down?理解在哪个环节中断？

The output产物

A journey & opportunity map旅程与机会地图

Follow the task across discovery, setup, first value, everyday use and support.沿着发现、设置、首次价值、日常使用与支持，追踪完整任务。

The checkpoint检查点

Choose the moment that matters to the user.优先解决真正影响用户的关键时刻。

04 / 07 · Frame

What should this experience make possible?这段体验应该让什么成为可能？

The output产物

An experience thesis一句体验命题

Connect a useful promise with a credible mechanism and a meaningful action.用可信的机制，将有用的承诺连接到有意义的行动。

The checkpoint检查点

Useful. Credible. Distinctive. Human. Sustainable.有用、可信、独特、有感、可持续。

05 / 07 · Orchestrate

How does one touchpoint prepare the next?一个触点如何为下一个做好准备？

The output产物

A service & interaction blueprint服务与交互蓝图

Align the interface, the people, the information and the recovery path.让界面、人员、信息与异常恢复路径彼此衔接。

The checkpoint检查点

Context survives each handoff, including when something fails.每一次交接都保留上下文，也覆盖异常情况。

06 / 07 · Deliver

Can a person actually complete the task?用户真的能完成任务吗？

The output产物

An experience release plan体验发布计划

Prepare the product, guidance, language, support and feedback together.让产品、指引、语言、支持与反馈机制一同就绪。

The checkpoint检查点

The core path works. Claims are supported. Recovery is possible.关键路径可完成，主张有依据，异常可恢复。

07 / 07 · Learn

What did people understand—and do next?用户理解了什么，又采取了什么行动？

The output产物

A learning record学习记录

Compare observations with the original hypothesis. Feed the result back into the system.将观察与原始假设对照，把结果反馈到整个系统。

The checkpoint检查点

Make an explicit decision: scale, iterate, hold or stop.作出明确决策：扩大、迭代、暂缓或停止。

Seven stages. One learning loop. Move between them as the evidence changes.七个阶段，一个学习闭环。随着证据变化，随时回到需要重新思考的阶段。

03 / Interface DNA界面基因

The interface
serves the judgment.界面服务于判断。

Show the state.状态清晰。

Make source, context and limits visible where the decision happens.在用户作出决定的地方，呈现来源、情境与限制。

Suggest a next step.行动明确。

Explain what a signal can help someone explore next.说明一个信号能帮助用户继续探索什么。

Leave room for taste.保留感官判断。

Data supports experience. The person remains the judge.数据支持经验，判断仍由人作出。

Data is a clue.
Understanding is the value.数据是线索，理解才是价值。

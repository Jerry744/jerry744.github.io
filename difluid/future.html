06 / The long view长期视角

A living system.
A longer horizon.持续演进的系统，面向更长的未来。

Build a shared foundation. Try it in the real world. Keep what helps people understand.建立共同基础，在真实世界试用，保留真正帮助人们理解的部分。

Working proposal前瞻提案

A proposed operating rhythm for the brand and experience system. Timings are planning windows, not a launch commitment or an approved company policy.以下为品牌与体验系统的运营节奏建议。时间表示规划窗口，不构成上市承诺或已批准的公司制度。

01 / Establish the baseline建立基线

14 working days.
One shared foundation.14 个工作日，建立共同基础。

01—02

Decide the essentials.明确核心选择。

Document the brand story and the essential visual rules.记录品牌叙事与关键视觉规则。

03—05

Make the story usable.让叙事可被使用。

Build a company introduction that can be reviewed as a complete story.形成叙事完整、可供评审的公司介绍。

06—10

Turn rules into tools.把规则变成工具。

Translate the baseline into guidelines and foundational UI components.将基线转化为应用指南与基础 UI 组件。

11—14

Prove it in application.在应用中检验。

Apply the system to a product sheet, check consistency and prepare the handoff.用产品资料页验证系统，检查一致性并完成交接。

A reusable baseline is the first milestone. Real-world use is the next test.可复用的基线是第一个里程碑，真实使用是下一次检验。

6–10

weeks / pilot window周 / 建议试点周期

02 / Start with one journey从一条旅程开始

Small enough to learn.
Real enough to matter.小到足以学习，真实到有意义。

Choose a bounded journey—from understanding a product to reaching its first useful result. Connect device or software, guidance and service around the same task.选择一条范围明确的旅程：从理解产品，到首次获得有用的结果。让设备或软件、指引与服务围绕同一个任务衔接。

Before the pilot试点之前

Record the baseline, the hypothesis and what would make us stop.记录基线、假设与停止条件。

During the pilot试点之中

Observe real tasks. Capture confusion as carefully as success.观察真实任务，同样认真地记录困惑与成功。

After the pilot试点之后

Decide what to change, what to reuse and what to retire.决定修改什么、复用什么，以及停止什么。

03 / A rhythm for learning学习的节奏

A release is
a beginning.发布，是一个开始。

T is the pilot release date; +14 and +45 mean days after release. Each review should end with a decision.T 指试点发布日期；+14 与 +45 分别指发布后 14 天与 45 天。每一次复盘，都应该形成一个决策。

  1. T+14

    Does it make sense?用户理解了吗？

    Review confusion, broken paths and early actions. Resolve the obstacles to first value.检查理解偏差、路径故障与早期行动，解决首次获得价值的障碍。

  2. T+45

    Does it keep helping?它持续有用吗？

    Review adoption, repeated use and continuity across touchpoints.检查采用、持续使用与跨触点的连续性。

  3. Quarterly每季度

    What should evolve?什么应该继续演进？

    Scale, iterate, hold or stop. Update the standards and knowledge that the next team will use.扩大、迭代、暂缓或停止，并更新下一次工作要使用的标准与知识。

04 / Define before measuring先定义，再衡量

Measure understanding.
Then what it enables.衡量理解，以及理解带来的改变。

Proposed measures, not reported results. Establish a baseline before setting targets.以下是建议指标，并非已取得的成果。先建立基线，再设定目标。

Understanding理解+

Explanation accuracy复述正确率

The share of participants who correctly explain the meaning and limits of a result in a defined task.在指定任务中，能够正确解释结果含义与适用限制的参与者比例。

Use a consistent prompt and a documented scoring rubric.使用一致的问题与明确的评分标准。

Action行动+

Task success任务成功率

The share of task attempts completed against predefined success criteria.满足预先定义成功标准的任务尝试，占全部任务尝试的比例。

Define the task, participants and allowed assistance before testing.测试前明确任务、参与者与允许的协助程度。

Continuity连续性+

Handoff completion交接完成率

The share of observed touchpoint transitions that preserve the context required for the next step.在观察到的触点交接中，保留了下一步所需上下文的交接比例。

Specify the required context for each transition in advance.提前定义每一次交接必须保留的上下文。

System health系统健康+

Release readiness发布就绪率

The share of required readiness checks that pass before a scoped release.在指定发布范围内，发布前通过的必需就绪检查占比。

Keep critical failures visible; an average must not hide a blocked path.单独呈现关键失败项，避免平均值掩盖无法完成的路径。

A brand becomes stronger
with every useful lesson.每一次有用的学习，都让品牌更有力量。
